There are three kinds of dropdown here. Your current choices are shown underneath.

## The dropdowns

- The first two are native `<select>` elements, so most frameworks can choose an option directly by its value or text; the second allows several options to be picked at once.
- The third is a custom dropdown built from buttons and a list: it is not a `<select>`, and its options only exist in the DOM, at the end of the page, while it is open, so click it to open it and then click an option.
- Some options are disabled and cannot be picked.
